    Welcome to SHUTTLEFEED

    A multi-mode automated shuttlecock feeder kiosk for PHED 4 at Cavite State University – Carmona Campus.

    🎯
    Consistent feedingNo arm fatigue, so every shuttle is delivered the same way.
    🏸
    Two drill modesSmash (40–70 m/s) and Service, aimed left or right.
    🎛️
    Simple controlsSet range, direction, angle, interval and shuttle count in one screen.
    Why SHUTTLEFEED?

    Badminton is a core part of PHED 4, and mastering services and smashes takes a lot of repetition. Manual feeding by peers or instructors is predictable, tiring, and becomes inconsistent over time. It also keeps students from practicing on their own.

    The problem we address

    Traditional feeding has limited variety and predictable patterns, and instructor fatigue makes delivery inconsistent. Commercial launchers are often expensive. SHUTTLEFEED offers automated, consistent feeding in Smash and Service modes, controlled from a simple kiosk for the instructor.
