    Welcome to SHUTTLEFEED

    A multi-mode automated shuttlecock feeder kiosk for PHED 4 at Cavite State University – Carmona Campus.

    🎯
    Consistent feedingNo arm fatigue, so every shuttle is delivered the same way.
    🏸
    Three drill modesHigh Angle, Rally and Service. High Angle and Rally run at one fixed speed; Service has a short or long range. All can be aimed left, center or right.
    🎛️
    Simple controlsChoose the direction (and the range in Service) on one screen. Every drill runs 16 shuttles, one every 3 seconds.
